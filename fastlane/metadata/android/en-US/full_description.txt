Brazier helps you track the true monthly cost of your physical items and purchases over time.

Instead of only looking at the initial purchase price, Brazier calculates how much an item actually costs you per month based on how long you have owned and used it.

Key Features:
• Item Cost Tracking: See both your target goal rate and your actual used cost per month.
• Monthly Review Tab: Swipe items right (used) or left (not used) each month with smooth animations and tactile haptic feedback.
• Usage Streak & Badges: Monitor consecutive months of usage and earn winstreak badges as items reach their cost targets.
• Overview & Statistics: View total expenditure, average cost per item, goal completion rates, and winstreak rankings.
• Custom Categories: Manage category icons, names, and custom colors.
• Clean & Private: 100% offline, privacy-first, zero analytics, zero ads, and zero tracking. All data stays strictly on your device.
• Material 3 Design: Full dark mode and adaptive modern UI.
