# Privacy Policy for Brazier

**Effective Date:** February 24, 2025  
**Last Updated:** February 24, 2025  

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Brazier** mobile application (the "App"). Brazier is designed as a privacy-first application to help users track the true monthly cost and usage of their personal items.

Your privacy is paramount. Brazier is built with a **privacy-by-design** principle: it functions 100% offline, collects no personal information, contains no advertisements, and does not track user behavior or device analytics.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Brazier does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive user information, or device telemetry.**

### Data Stored Locally
When you use Brazier, all data you input—including:
* Item names, purchase prices, target goal rates, and notes
* Custom category names, icons, and colors
* Monthly usage logs, review records, and streak counts
* App theme preferences and settings

...is saved **exclusively on your local device**. 

### Internet Access
Brazier does **not** request or use internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`). The App operates entirely offline and has no ability or mechanism to connect to remote servers or transmit data off your device.

---

## 2. Data Storage and Security

* **Local Storage:** All app data is saved locally using Android's standard local key-value storage (`SharedPreferences`).
* **Security:** Because all data remains locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security controls (e.g., lock screen passcode, biometrics, device encryption).
* **No Remote Servers or Cloud Sync:** We do not operate external databases, cloud servers, or remote backups for your data.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services, SDKs, and Advertising

Brazier does **not** integrate or use any third-party tools, advertising networks, or analytics SDKs, including:
* **No Advertising Frameworks** (e.g., Google AdMob)
* **No Analytics or Telemetry** (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics)
* **No Crash Reporting Frameworks** (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
* **No Third-Party Authentication** or User Tracking Services

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Brazier does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the App collects zero personal data, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## 5. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

You have complete control over all data generated within Brazier:

* **In-App Management:** You can edit or delete individual items, categories, or usage records at any time within the App.
* **Complete Data Wipe:** You can permanently delete all data stored by Brazier at any time by:
  1. Navigating to your device **Settings** > **Apps** > **Brazier** > **Storage** > **Clear Data**.
  2. Uninstalling the Brazier application from your device.

---

## 6. System Permissions

Brazier requests zero sensitive system permissions. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, photo library, microphone, or hardware identifiers.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Since Brazier does not collect user contact information, you are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
